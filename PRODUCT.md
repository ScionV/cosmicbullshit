# Cosmic Bullshit

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Astro with TypeScript, confirmed by the user. Static Cloudflare deployment linked to GitHub (ScionV/cosmicbullshit). Generate factual event data ahead of time with pyswisseph rather than running Python per visit. User chose AGPL-3.0 and open-source publication for the project.

## Users

People looking for a humorous cosmic explanation for their everyday problems. More specific audience characteristics remain undecided.

## Product Purpose

A tongue-in-cheek astrology website that gives people astrological phenomena they can blame their issues on, such as Mercury in retrograde. The central experience is finding an amusing cosmic excuse for a problem.

## Positioning

The premise is cosmic blame as entertainment. Astrology supplies the material for the joke. Specific differentiating features remain undecided.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Cite astrological phenomena as humorous explanations for everyday issues.
- The homepage has one primary button: give the visitor something cosmic to blame. Problem entry and category selection are not required for this experience.
- Excuses mix real celestial events and invented nonsense, with clear labels distinguishing them. Use pyswisseph for factual events, retain calculation metadata and UTC validity windows, and fall back to invented excuses outside the available data range.
- Deploy through Cloudflare linked to GitHub. Persistence, accounts, monetization, and other integrations have not been specified.
- The clerk rests as a still image, with a varied idle reaction after a random 15–30 second pause. A blame request takes priority and triggers reading, dispensing, and a readable fortune. Reduced-motion users receive the still-and-text experience.

## Brand Commitments

- The user has secured cosmicbullshit.com.
- Working product name: Cosmic Bullshit, drawn from that domain.
- Tone: playful and absurd, with occasional swearing, grounded in the cosmic-blame premise.
- Approved character: a frazzled cosmic bureaucrat in a wrinkled, ill-fitting paisley suit and disheveled mystic cap, inside a plum-and-brass fortune machine.
- The original domain idea, whatcosmicbullshitcanIblamemyproblemson.com, expresses the intended product question; the secured domain is cosmicbullshit.com.

## Product Principles

- Keep finding a cosmic excuse central to the experience.
- Preserve the tongue-in-cheek treatment of astrology.
- Use the shorter secured domain as the product's home.
